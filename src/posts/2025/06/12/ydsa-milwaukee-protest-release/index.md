MILWAUKEE — The Milwaukee Young Democratic Socialists of America (MKE YDSA) joined hundreds of protesters Tuesday to march against President Donald Trump’s immigration policies, in solidarity with Los Angeles demonstrations.

Demonstrators rallied at Cathedral Square Park before sweeping through downtown, arriving at the city’s Department of Homeland Security office on East Knapp Street. They were joined by countless community organizations, including three YDSA chapters from [the Milwaukee School of Engineering](https://www.instagram.com/msoe_ydsa/), [Marquette University](https://www.instagram.com/ydsa.marquette/) and [the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee](https://www.instagram.com/ydsauwm/).

With numberless picket signs, chants, and speeches, the demonstrators loudly rejected the Trump Administration’s unprecedented deportation efforts. As the march passed highrise apartments, Milwaukeeans shouted their support from balconies above while the spirit of the anti-Trump movement was captured in one roaring chant: “one struggle, one fight.”

“Milwaukee definitely could show more,” said Isaiah Clark, a member of the YDSA chapter at UW-Milwaukee. “That being said, I am proud of the turnout.”

As did many other major cities across the United States, Milwaukee also stood in solidarity with anti-Immigration-and-Customs-Enforcement protests in Los Angeles as Trump deployed hundreds of [US Marines and the National Guard over the weekend, Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/6/8/trump-deployed-national-guard-arrives-in-la-to-crush-immigration-protests).

“ICE is here in Wisconsin too,” said Fernanda Jimenez of Comité Sin Fronteras, “They’re stalking our neighborhoods, threatening our families and they are waiting outside of homes to kidnap our people.”

None of us are free until all of us are. If you are a student at MSOE, Marquette, or UW-Milwaukee, join your school’s YDSA chapter today to get involved in the struggle for immigrants’ rights. Follow their Instagram pages to get the latest meeting information:

- MSOE YDSA: <https://www.instagram.com/msoe_ydsa/>
- Marquette YDSA: <https://www.instagram.com/ydsa.marquette/>
- UW-Milwaukee: <https://www.instagram.com/uwmydsa/>

This is a fight we *all* must join, not just students. The Milwaukee Democratic Socialists of America is committed to building a mass movement for an equitable, just society, standing with any and all groups targeted by the Trump Administration.

## About MKE YDSA

[The Milwaukee Young Democratic Socialists of America](https://mkeydsa.org/) is an autonomous and democratically organized chapter of the Young Democratic Socialists of America – the student and youth wing of the Democratic Socialists of America. MKE YDSA has branches at Milwaukee School of Engineering, Marquette University, and the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. Members of MKE YDSA are students in Milwaukee organizing to build a better world.

## About YDSA

[The Young Democratic Socialists of America](https://y.dsausa.org/) is the youth and student section of the Democratic Socialists of America, and a national organization of recognized campus chapters and several hundred activists. We are students organizing in our universities, colleges, and high schools to fight for the immediate needs of workers and students while building our capacity to fight for more radical and structural changes.
