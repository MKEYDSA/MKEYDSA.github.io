Students: Vote For Alex Brower on April 1st!

by Outreach Committee

March 25, 2025

YDSA@UWM and MKE YDSA are proud to endorse Alex Brower for Common Council!

The Young Democratic Socialists of America chapter at UW-Milwaukee proudly endorses Alex Brower for Milwaukee’s 3rd Common Council district. Alex is committed to the improvement of the lives of regular people on Milwaukee's East Side. He understands what it takes to fight for real change in our schools and in our workplaces. He is a progressive, and he is earnestly dedicated to making our lives better. As co-chair of the Milwaukee Democratic Socialists of America, Alex actively fostered the growth and development of our chapter, ensuring the next generation of progressives are ready for the fight that lies ahead. This is the wisdom and leadership Milwaukee needs.

We firmly believe that Alex will fight for working class issues. We support him as students for his unwavering support for public education and the youth movement. We support him as socialists for his promise to fight for publicly owned electricity to get our utility bills down. And we support him as young workers for his championing of workers’ rights.

Want to get involved? Join us for canvassing from 11 AM to 1 PM on Wednesday, March 26th and Monday, March 31st. Canvassing will kick off at Zao MKE (2319 E Kenwood Blvd, Milwaukee, WI 53211).

Get out there and vote on April 1st! Find your polling place at mkeydsa.org/vote.

About YDSA@UWM

YDSA@UWM is an autonomous and democratically organized branch of the Young Democratic Socialists, the student and youth wing of the Democratic Socialists of America. Members of YDSA@UWM are students and workers at UW-Milwaukee organizing to build a better world.

About YDSA

The Young Democratic Socialists of America is the youth and student section of the Democratic Socialists of America, and a national organization of recognized campus chapters and several hundred activists. We are students organizing in our universities, colleges, and high schools to fight for the immediate needs of workers and students while building our capacity to fight for more radical and structural changes.
